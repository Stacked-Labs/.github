# Your table. Your money.

Stacked is a player-hosted online poker app. Every table is created and run by a player — the **Host** — and the money never sits with us: your USDC lives in that table's own smart contract on [Base](https://base.org) (a low-fee, Ethereum-based network), and only you can move it.

### Why players trust it

- **Your table.** Every game is created and hosted by a real player. Even public tables need the Host's approval before you sit down — it's a home game, not a faceless lobby.
- **Your money.** Your buy-in sits in the table's own on-chain contract, not a house account. You sign your own deposits and withdrawals.
- **Your way out.** If settlement ever stalls, you can self-withdraw your funds after 24 hours — no permission needed, no support ticket.
- **Two systems, no smoke.** Our servers run the live game; contracts on Base hold the money and settle every hand, typically in under 5 seconds. We built it that way, and we say so plainly.

### What's live today

- **Cash games** — Texas Hold'em, real USDC
- **Tournaments** — customizable, with payouts that grow as the field does
- **Free Play** — zero-risk practice tables in the same lobby, clearly tagged
- **Host earnings** — anyone can open a table and earn a share of the platform fee
- **Free-entry tickets & referrals** — Hosts can send friends in for free
